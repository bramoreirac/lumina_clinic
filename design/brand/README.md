# Reconstructed Lúmina logo

These files are a working reconstruction from the customer-supplied `customer_files/content/video01.mov`, sampled at approximately 00:24.2. The source image is a compressed video frame, so this is an approximation rather than the original master artwork. The flower and lettering were traced as vector paths; text is not dependent on a font being installed.

- `lumina-logo-reconstructed.svg`: scalable vector with a transparent background and accessible title/description.
- `lumina-logo-reconstructed.png`: transparent 1500 × 1042 pixel export of the same vector.
- `lumina-logo-ink.svg`: monochrome deep-black (`#17181A`) version for light backgrounds, derived from the same vector paths.

The gold SVG and PNG use `#B48F2D`, an approximation sampled from the video's logo, rather than the brand manual's `#C9A66B` accent. This was chosen to preserve the reference's appearance; the final logo color should be checked against the original artwork when available. The traced contours have been simplified and fitted with smooth quadratic curves to remove video-pixel stair stepping while retaining intentional corners. Both SVG variants share the same geometry; the transparent PNG is rendered from the gold SVG at 1500 ? 1042 pixels. The reconstruction still approximates the source lettering and flower rather than reproducing an original master exactly.

For legibility, the reconstructed gold has an approximate contrast ratio of 5.84:1 against the brand's deep black `#17181A` and 2.64:1 against ivory `#F2EFE6`. Prefer a dark background while retaining this color, especially where the small “Clínica Estética” lettering must be readable. Do not stretch the logo or use the full lockup at a size where the small text disappears.

The supplied video and extracted working frame remain unchanged. The clinic preview uses the gold reconstruction in its dark opening and the ink version on its light header and footer. This reconstructed artwork remains provisional; if the customer later supplies original SVG, PDF, or transparent high-resolution artwork, compare it and prefer the original master.
